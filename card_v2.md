**Title:** Green Cools the City: Vegetation and Surface Heat in 8 Bangladeshi Cities, 2014–2025

**Summary:** I compared vegetation (NDVI) with land surface temperature pixel by pixel in 95 pre-monsoon Landsat 8/9 scenes covering Bangladesh's eight divisional cities from 2014 to 2025. In every city and every year greener ground was cooler, by a median of 0.98 °C for each +0.1 NDVI, strongest in Rajshahi (1.56 °C) and weakest in Chattogram (0.76 °C). The result is an animated chart, an interactive swipe map with a linked pixel scatter, and a reproducible Python pipeline.
